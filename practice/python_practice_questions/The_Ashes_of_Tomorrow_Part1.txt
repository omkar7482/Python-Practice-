Title: The Ashes of Tomorrow

In the year 2147, the world as humanity once knew it has vanished. Cities lie in ruin, buried under storms of ash and silence. Civilization ended not with a bang, but with a whisper — the whisper of hunger, dust, and betrayal.

This is the story of those who refused to fade away. Of the survivors who dared to dream of rebuilding. Of fire born from ruin.

Chapter 1: The Silent City

As the world burned, survivors clung to fragments of memory. In the hollow streets of the fallen megacities, voices still echoed — reminders of what once was. Chapter 1 follows a band of wanderers searching for a place called Haven, a mythical city said to still have light.

The air was thick with radioactive fog. Each step they took left footprints that glowed faintly, like ghosts refusing to die. Among them was Mira, a former engineer turned scavenger, and Dax, a soldier haunted by the past. Their goal: to find the key to restarting the old power grids — a spark that could bring warmth to the endless night.
